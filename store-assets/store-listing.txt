SHORT DESCRIPTION
Share photos & videos, join live voice rooms, go live and send gifts.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Rara Live is a social app where you can share photo and video posts, talk in live voice rooms, go live on video and celebrate with people in real time.

📸 PHOTO & VIDEO POSTS
Share your moments with the community. Pick photos and videos from your gallery, add up to 10 in one post with a caption, and publish to the Home feed. Scroll the feed, like and comment on posts, follow creators, and see everyone's posts on their profile.

🎙️ LIVE VOICE ROOMS
Create your own room or join one that matches your interests. Take a mic seat, talk with everyone in the room and invite friends to join the conversation. Hosts can manage seats, mute, remove or block people to keep their room friendly.

📹 GO LIVE
Start a live video stream in seconds. Talk with your viewers, read their comments in real time and invite co-hosts on stage.

⚔️ PK BATTLES
Challenge other hosts to live PK battles. Supporters send gifts to cheer for their favourite host, and the scoreboard updates live.

🎁 GIFTS & COINS
Send animated gifts to show your appreciation for hosts and friends. Gift combos and effects make every moment in the room feel special.

💬 CHAT & CALLS
Message friends privately with text, photos, videos and voice messages. Friend Zone lets you have one-to-one audio conversations with listed members.

🏆 LEVELS & LEADERBOARDS
Earn XP, level up, complete daily tasks and climb the daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards.

🌐 YOUR LANGUAGE
Use Rara Live in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali.

🛡️ SAFE COMMUNITY
Rara Live is for adults aged 18 and above. You can report any user from their profile, and room hosts can remove and block people. Nudity, harassment, hate speech and any form of child exploitation are strictly prohibited and lead to permanent bans.
